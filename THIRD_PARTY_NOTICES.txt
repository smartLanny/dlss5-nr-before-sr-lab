NVIDIA and games
----------------

The source repository contains no NVIDIA SDK library/header, neural-network
payload, game file, or game asset. Production nrchain_nvngx.dll binaries link
the official NVIDIA NGX SDK loader in object-code form to initialize NGX in
applications that have not initialized it themselves. The SDK is separately
obtained by the builder from https://github.com/NVIDIA/DLSS (pinned build inputs
are recorded in scripts/build-nrchain.ps1). NVIDIA SDK portions remain governed
by the NVIDIA RTX SDKs license at https://github.com/NVIDIA/DLSS/blob/a291cc7d2cc642a51566f3dfd5376f635cd1b284/LICENSE.txt;
they are not relicensed under this project's license. The signed NR runtime is
supplied separately. Product names describe interoperability and do not imply
affiliation or endorsement.
